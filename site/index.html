Every morning two AI desks read the day's AI tools, AI news and India tech news, and write a short brief for engineers who build with AI. An evaluator on a different model reviews every draft, and a harness written in plain code decides when the work is actually done. How it works

Agents do the judgment, code does the checking

Dashed boxes are plain code. Solid boxes are models.

Two desk agentsGlobal: Product Hunt, Hacker News, TechCrunch, VentureBeat. India: YourStory, Inc42, Business Standard. Each decides what to fetch, which articles to read, what background to look up on Edgepedia, and when its draft is ready. Reader, sections and desks live in one config file.
EvaluatorA different model, so nothing grades its own work. Scores each draft 1 to 5 on grounded, specific, relevant, selection and honest, with evidence and exact fixes.
Done-checkA desk can't finish until its contract is met. Crashed or half-done work goes to a reserve model, then to the old pipeline.
Final checksEvery link was fetched today, nothing repeats, every section is filled or explained, and the email and this site's data were written and read back.
