Jarvis · multi-agent news system

Jarvis AI Agent

Every morning two AI desks read the day's AI and startup news and write a digest for someone breaking into product management. An evaluator on a different model reviews every draft, and a harness written in plain code decides when the work is actually done. This site shows each run with its evidence: what the agents did, what the evaluator rejected, and every check the harness ran.

How it works

Agents do the judgment, code does the checking

Dashed boxes are plain code. Solid boxes are models.

Two desk agentsGlobal: Product Hunt, Hacker News, TechCrunch, VentureBeat. India: YourStory, Inc42, Business Standard. Each decides what to fetch, which articles to read and when its draft is ready.
EvaluatorA different model, so nothing grades its own work. Scores each draft 1 to 5 on grounded, specific, relevant, selection and honest, with evidence and exact fixes.
Done-checkA desk can't finish until its contract is met. Crashed or half-done work goes to a reserve model, then to the old pipeline.
Final checksEvery link was fetched today, nothing repeats, every section is filled or explained, and the email and this site's data were written and read back.
